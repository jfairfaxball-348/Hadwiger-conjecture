# Open frontier

Research cutoff: 2026-09-30. Session: RL2. Status: NOT PROMOTED — working research checkpoint.

The highest-level, most demanding target for this project is the unrestricted sharp conjecture: for every finite simple G and integer t>=1, chi(G)>=t forces a complete t-branch-set minor model. This includes unbounded t, arbitrary graph order, and simultaneous compatibility of every branch set. It is the root objective, not a claimed formal ordering of the difficulty of all open problems. [RES-0001, RES-0002; OPEN-0001]

The first unresolved fixed case is t=7. It is an important landmark, but solving it alone would leave the unbounded-parameter target open. The known t=6 reduction cannot simply be extrapolated. [RES-0005, RES-0006; OPEN-0002]
