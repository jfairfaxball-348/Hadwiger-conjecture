The final countermodel in the verdict is especially instructive. The retained height profile satisfies the stated structural counts, least-root window and full-period affine recurrence, yet its closing root is nonintegral. Satisfying a long list of necessary conditions did not supply the missing arithmetic sufficiency condition. For Hadwiger, a collection of density, connectivity, local models or special-class colorings must similarly not be reported as a complete clique-minor model.

The transferable standard is to state the bridge, try to break its sufficiency at its exact scope, preserve the outcome, and only then justify dependent work. A counterexample to the root conjecture must satisfy its full premise and contradict its full conclusion; a countermodel to weaker bridge antecedents only rejects that proposed route.
