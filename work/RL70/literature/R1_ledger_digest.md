## Part C — detail paragraphs for FL-055..FL-070

**FL-055 (RL54) — K4,4 payoff barrier.** SRC-0025 (the "any 7-chromatic graph has K7 or K4,4 as a minor" title theorem; the ledger does not name authors — the RL63 attack map attributes the K4,4 residue to "KT") was upgraded to checked_primary at theorem-statement level from the Springer record; the full proof was NOT reconstructed (PK-3 note restores this caveat, which the consolidated text had dropped). So every HC7 counterexample has a K4,4 minor, but K4,4 is 2-colourable and delta(G) >= 8 does not pass to the contracted quotient. Barred moves: treating the minor as a subgraph, induced subgraph, separator, prescribed model or independent bipartite side. Retry: arbitrary legitimate model, every normalisation proved.
