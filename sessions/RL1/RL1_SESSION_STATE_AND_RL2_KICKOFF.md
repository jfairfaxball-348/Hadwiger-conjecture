## RL2 purpose

RL2 is the **background research / literature intelligence session**.

Its job is to deeply map the existing research landscape around the Hadwiger Conjecture and leave a durable, source-grounded, machine-readable research corpus so later sessions can retrieve known results, terminology, variants, proof techniques, open cases, and provenance from the repository instead of repeatedly rediscovering them.

RL2 is not a proof-attack session. It may explain and organize existing mathematics, but it should not spend the session attempting a new proof of the conjecture.

The binding RL2 instructions are carried by successor `authoritative/START_HERE.md`, `RL2_BACKGROUND_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md`, and `RL2_RESEARCH_RECORD_SPEC.json`.
