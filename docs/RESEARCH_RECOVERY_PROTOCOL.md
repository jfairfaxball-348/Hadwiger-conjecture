# Repair, rework, and pivot protocol

`AGENTS.md` is binding. Adopted by direct user instruction on 2026-09-30: this is a long programme with dead ends and failures; preserve mistakes and issues, learn from them, and build the ability to repair, rework, or pivot into the process.

## Scope and two admission gates

Full sharp Hadwiger remains the root: h(G)>=chi(G) for every finite simple graph, with both values zero on the empty graph. Either a full rigorous proof or an actual rigorously verified finite counterexample is legitimate. A bridge countermodel has its own scope and is not automatically a root counterexample.

**Exploration admission:** a worker may propose and investigate an unproved input if it names a concrete mechanism, explains its relevance to a recorded missing dependency, distinguishes established facts from hypotheses, and supplies a bounded test. The user need not provide a theorem or candidate graph first. Candidate formulation and bounded falsification can themselves be research tasks.

**Proof admission:** dependent conclusions may consume an input only after its exact quantifiers, provenance, proof or complete certificate, applicability, and sufficiency are justified. A conditional implication may be recorded as conditional while its input remains open. Independent justification, exhaustive coverage, simultaneous model compatibility, retained sharpness, and non-circularity remain mandatory for an unconditional conclusion.

This separation permits discovery without relaxing mathematical standards. A route that is merely equivalent to the root can orient thinking but closes no obligation. Renaming it does not qualify as a changed mechanism.

## Recovery cycle

1. **Isolate.** Stop deductions that use the failed step. Freeze the last unquestionably valid frontier and distinguish error, countermodel, coverage barrier, inconclusive result, access/tool defect, and process defect. An integrity failure requires stop-and-repair; an explicit source gap is an obligation, not an integrity failure.
2. **Preserve.** Add an entry/event to `authoritative/FAILURE_AND_LESSON_LEDGER.md`, with original expectation, evidence and exact version/path, first invalid or missing dependency, downstream impact, surviving valid scope, lesson, and retry conditions. Work records remain unpromoted until the normal atomic RL closeout. Keep failed attempts and original source/access logs; never edit a frozen session to improve its history.
3. **Diagnose.** Explain which specific condition failed: antecedent, all-colorings quantifier, coverage, extraction/preservation, compatibility, sharpness, circularity, source applicability, reproducibility, or tooling. State what would change the diagnosis and what would not. A valid restricted theorem can be irrelevant without being erroneous.
4. **Choose.** Compare at most three concrete candidates in one recovery work unit. Prefer an obstruction-changing repair; otherwise rework the interface or pivot to a different inherited bridge. Choose one candidate for deeper assessment. If every candidate is rejected, preserve the reasons and identify one different bounded candidate-development task for the next unit, rather than repeating them or inventing progress.
5. **Assess.** Record exact inputs, full candidate quantifiers, status, root relevance, expected output, bounds, falsification test, and stopping criteria before consequences. Do one coherent bounded assessment. Any new search, experiment, or lemma attempt needs a named question and a reason it can change the existing frontier.
6. **Learn and hand over.** Record the outcome and classify it honestly. Explain which obligation was reduced, if any; otherwise record the barrier learned. Update the ledger and durable resume task. Recommend continue or finish under AGENTS.md. Closing the work unit or RL leaves the programme active with a portable next task unless the user pauses or ends it.

## Recovery modes

| Mode | Required change | Inadmissible substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Repair | Correct or independently justify the first failed dependency; recheck every affected conclusion. | Quietly removing a hypothesis or treating an invalidated conclusion as proved. |
| Rework | Replace the mechanism, interface, or decomposition while retaining a proved connection to the root; expose all residual cases. | More consequences of a mechanism known to cover no relevant cases. |
| Pivot | Choose a different bridge or a concrete candidate-negative strategy with exact root relevance and testable work. | An easier variant substituted for full Hadwiger, or an unbounded roadmap refresh. |

Before restarting a stopped mechanism, state the **change from the recorded attempt** and show how it addresses the recorded obstruction. A newly formulated input can be assessed as a candidate; it cannot yet license its consequences. A new title, extra local conditions, repeated query, or unchanged inaccessible URL is not a change.

## Required failure/lesson record

Use stable IDs and append dated outcome events. Every record carries:

- originating session/attempt and exact artifact, source version, or certificate;
- claim/expectation and proof status before the issue;
- actual observation, its verification scope, and remaining uncertainty;
- first invalid or missing dependency and affected downstream claims;
- last valid frontier, surviving results, uncovered cases/parameters, and retained source gaps;
- diagnosis and a concrete lesson applied to the next candidate;
- disposition: repair needed, active candidate, suspended, retired at a stated scope, or resolved at a stated scope;
- retry condition, next bounded task, and subsequent events, including any correction/demotion.

Record a mistake plainly when evidence establishes it. Do not retroactively label a valid scoped theorem as a false claim, or a failed retrieval as mathematical falsification. Link imported failure reviews at their inspected scope; do not upgrade their verification status.

## Bounds and anti-repetition

Each continuation remains one bounded work unit. Write numeric limits for searches or computations before starting them; a desk-based assessment may instead fix the exact files, candidate count, and one target inference. Incomplete runs retain exact uncovered ranges and are NOT PROMOTED. Reserve closeout capacity.

Record attempted candidates and rejected inputs. A retry must identify new information, a changed premise/mechanism, or a materially different falsification domain. A failed test stops that candidate's dependent deductions immediately. No automatic broad search loop, expanding census, consequence stack, or repeated administrative recovery session is justified by the programme's persistence.

If no useful changed candidate is available, report that exact limitation and hand over a bounded task to develop one at a specified missing bridge. If that task also fails, preserve the result and reassess another bridge; do not silently terminate the programme or promise a mathematical breakthrough. Every finite unit may end inconclusively.

## Handover and safeguards

Carry the ledger, last valid frontier, failed/rejected attempt IDs, exact gaps, and one next recovery task into each successor. Recovery changes research decisions, not the classification of inherited mathematics. Explicit corrections/demotions and their downstream rechecks remain mandatory when actual errors are found.

An unresolved start-gate/integrity defect prevents ordinary mathematics until repaired. A pivot cannot evade it. User-directed pauses, finite work-unit bounds, and CLOSEOUT_LOCK remain binding. During closeout, preserve the already established recovery frontier; do not start another assessment.

The process may be amended in an explicitly authorized non-RL commit. Such an amendment keeps the incoming RL number and frozen history, records changed control instructions, and promotes no theorem, certificate, source resolution, or research result.
