## New scoped analytic result

RL14-P01 proves an exact obstruction to the prepared leaf-insertion mechanism. In any actual proper six-coloring d of G-x, every color appears on N_G(x). For any two colors p,q, some p/q Kempe component meets N_G(x) in both colors; otherwise swapping all p/q components that meet the p-colored neighbors would free p for x and six-color G.

With gamma=d(v), a single gamma/delta component swap makes gamma absent from N_G(x) exactly when one component contains every gamma-neighbor of x and no delta-neighbor of x. The critical Kempe-lock lemma makes that conjunction impossible for every delta!=gamma. Therefore the prepared one-component insertion mechanism fails for all five palettes, even without requiring the swapped component to avoid v.
