# Contributing

Contributions, corrections, verifier improvements, computational experiments, and mathematical observations are welcome.

Please clearly classify claims as one of the categories in `docs/PROOF_STATE_CLASSIFICATIONS.md`, and include enough information for a claim or computation to be independently checked.

Do not treat a result as established merely because it appears in a research-session file.

When modifying an existing claim, preserve the research record by stating explicitly whether the claim is strengthened, weakened, corrected, demoted, or withdrawn. Do not silently rewrite historical status.

## Conveyor integrity

Numbered mathematical research transitions follow `AGENTS.md`.

Infrastructure changes should be separate from numbered RL transitions and must not silently change `authoritative/`, frozen `sessions/`, or recorded proof classifications.
