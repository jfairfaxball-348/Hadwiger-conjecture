# Proof-state classifications

`AGENTS.md` is binding. These labels are the canonical vocabulary for promoted research state.

- **Proved analytic mathematics** — a stated result with a complete proof and exact scope.
- **Exact finite certificate** — a gap-free, reproducible finite computation with exact scope and a verifier or independently checkable witness.
- **Externally inherited certificate** — a certificate accepted from a frozen earlier state with exact provenance retained.
- **Computational evidence** — observations or experiments that do not establish a theorem.
- **Conjecture / candidate lemma** — a proposed statement still requiring proof or disproof.
- **Method barrier / dead route** — a documented obstruction, failure mode, countermodel, or reason a strategy does not currently work.
- **Repaired or demoted claim** — an explicit correction identifying the invalid dependency, reason, and remaining valid scope.
- **Open obligation** — a condition still required for a named local or global conclusion.

No evidence becomes proof by accumulation alone. No incomplete range becomes an exact certificate. No branch-local result becomes global without a valid bridge. No correction may be silent.

## Canonical status sources

Proof status comes from current or frozen proof-state records, correction/demotion records, explicit session closeouts, or files that a handover explicitly designates as status-bearing.

Filenames, search snippets, index entries, prose summaries, and model judgement do not independently assign proof status.

If canonical sources conflict, preserve the conflict and mark it for mathematical review rather than silently adjudicating it during infrastructure work.
